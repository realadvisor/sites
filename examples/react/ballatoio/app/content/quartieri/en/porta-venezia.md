Liberty facades and late nineteenth-century buildings, with porters, stone staircases and generous flats. Towards Viale Piave and Via Melzo the streets are full of shops and bars; across the corso, towards Centrale station, it is quieter.

The M1 stops at Porta Venezia and Lima, and the suburban rail at Porta Venezia, reach the centre and Centrale in a few minutes.
