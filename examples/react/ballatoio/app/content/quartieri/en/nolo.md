NoLo, North of Loreto, is a mixed neighbourhood: early 1900s apartment buildings, railing houses, courtyards with workshops turned into lofts and studios. Prices are lower than in the centre and homes larger for the same budget.

The M1 and M2 metro lines cross at Loreto; the Martesana park follows the canal up to Gorla.
