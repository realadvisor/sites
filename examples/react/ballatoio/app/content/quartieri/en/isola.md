Past the Via Farini bridge and Garibaldi station, Isola has kept its low streets: railing houses, 1930s buildings, small shops and the odd old workshop turned into a loft. Further north, towards Piazzale Maciachini, homes are larger and more recent.

The M5 line stops at Isola and Garibaldi; the suburban rail and regional trains leave from Garibaldi.
