Close to the centre, around Via degli Orti and the Crocetta, there are period buildings with courtyards and gardens. Towards Corso Lodi and Lodi TIBB come 1960s blocks with terraces and high floors, and the new buildings around the old rail yard.

The M3 line stops at Crocetta, Porta Romana and Lodi TIBB.
