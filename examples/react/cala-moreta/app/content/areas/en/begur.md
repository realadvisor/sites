Begur looks out over the Mediterranean from a hill crowned by the ruins of its castle. The streets of the old centre rise and fall between stone houses and the colonial façades built by the *indianos* who came home from Cuba.

From the village, narrow roads wind down through the pines to small coves of clear water. Many of the villas we sell sit on those slopes, with terraces facing the sea and gardens that need little watering.

In winter Begur is quiet and keeps a life of its own: a market, a school and restaurants open all year. It is one of the reasons so many families decide to stay beyond the summer.
