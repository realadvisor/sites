Many of our buyers start with a rental. A few months in a village teach you things a viewing cannot: where to park in August, which bakery opens in January, how long the road to Girona takes.

## What to look for in a rental

A house in the village you are interested in, with a clear contract on the length, the deposit and the costs. We explain every point before you sign.

## And then

If the village wins you over, you will already know the streets and which house you are looking for. So will we.
