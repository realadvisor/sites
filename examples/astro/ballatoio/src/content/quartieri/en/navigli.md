Along the two canals there are railing houses, old warehouses and workshops, and a few 1970s buildings. In the evening the towpaths are busy: if you want quiet, look at the inner streets, towards Via Vigevano or Via Corsico, or at high floors.

The M2 stop at Porta Genova and tram 3 along the Pavese connect the area to the centre.
