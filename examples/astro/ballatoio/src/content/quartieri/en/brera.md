Eighteenth and nineteenth-century palazzi, inner courtyards and flats with high ceilings and plasterwork. Few homes come up for sale and they change hands slowly: often on the first or second floor, rarely with an original lift.

The Duomo, the Castello and Garibaldi station are a few minutes' walk. The nearest metro stops are Lanza (M2) and Montenapoleone (M3).
