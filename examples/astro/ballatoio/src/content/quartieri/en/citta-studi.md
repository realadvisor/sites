Città Studi is the neighbourhood of the Politecnico and the science faculties. Many buildings are post-war, with lifts, balconies and a porter; homes are bright and often have a cellar. Lettings to students and researchers are a large part of the market.

The M2 line stops at Piola and Lambrate; Lambrate station has trains to Venice and to Linate airport.
