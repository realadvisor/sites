Tamariu is a small village of Palafrugell, gathered around a cove with pines that reach almost to the water. It has a short promenade, a few terraces and boats pulled up on the sand.

The houses are spread over the slopes around the bay, many with terraces stepping down to the sea. It is a place for those who prefer calm: out of season you hear little more than the water.
