The old town of Pals is a medieval enclosure of golden stone, with walls, a clock tower and lanes you can walk in half an hour without hurrying. Around it lies the Empordà plain: rice fields, cereal fields and farmhouses standing alone.

Pals beach, long and open, is a few minutes away by car. Here you will mostly find houses with gardens, recent villas with views of the hills and estates with land: what people look for when they want space and quiet.
