Peratallada is one of the best preserved medieval villages of the Empordà, with cobbled streets, a moat cut into the rock and stone houses with inner courtyards.

All around are fields and farmhouses: stone houses with a history, thick walls and room to live all year, some twenty minutes from the sea.
