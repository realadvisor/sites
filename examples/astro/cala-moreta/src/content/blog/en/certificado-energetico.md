On every listing of a house for sale or to rent you will see a letter from A to G. It is the rating of the **energy certificate** (*certificado energético*).

## What it measures

The certificate estimates how much energy the house needs for heating, cooling and hot water, and the emissions that come with it. A is the most efficient and G the least.

## Who issues it

A qualified technician signs it after visiting the house. The owner must have it before advertising the house, and the buyer or tenant receives it with the contract.

## How to read it

An old stone house can have a low letter and still be lovely to live in; a recent villa usually has a high one. The letter is a starting point to talk about insulation, windows or solar panels.
