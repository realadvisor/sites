In November Begur changes pace. The coves are left to dog walkers and the people who swim all year. In the square, the bars put their chairs out in the midday sun.

It is the best time to view houses: the light is low and golden, the paths are empty and you see the village as it is when nobody is in a hurry.

If you are thinking of living here all year, come in winter. We will show you the village and the houses that are best lived in with the fire lit.
